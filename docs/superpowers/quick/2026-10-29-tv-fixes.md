# TV fixes and top-left clock

## Goal
On the Xiaomi TV Box S, held volume ramps, Restart app really restarts, the Settings buttons always
open a fresh page, and Home shows the clock and weather top left with the three apps centred in the
left column.

## Scope
`kevbox/buttons/ButtonRow.kt`, `kevbox/menu/AppMenu.kt`, `kevbox/restart/*`,
`res/xml/home_accessibility.xml`, `HomeAccessibilityService.kt`, a new `kevbox/keys/VolumeRepeat.kt`,
`index/IndexTopBar.kt`, `index/IndexScreen.kt`, `index/AppList.kt`, `index/IndexTokens.kt`,
`UPSTREAM-SYNC.md`, tests under `app/src/test/java/cloud/samlo/couch/kevbox/`.

**Gate:** `export ANDROID_HOME=$HOME/Android/Sdk && ./gradlew --no-daemon testReleaseUnitTest lintRelease assembleRelease && test $(stat -c %s app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk) -le 1048576 && bash kevbox/provision/provision-test.sh`

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC-1** The Settings button opens Android's main Settings page every time, including right after
  a hold-OK "App info" visit followed by Home (today it reopens that app's details page).
- **AC-2** The Wi-Fi and Bluetooth buttons open their own pages fresh in the same way.
- **AC-3** Back from a page opened by "App info" or "Restart app" returns to where it was opened
  from (Home or the drawer), not to Settings' main page.
- **AC-4** On the Xiaomi box (`com.android.tv.settings`, Google TV), "Restart app" on a running app
  presses Force stop and its OK, then reopens the app; on an already-stopped app it reopens it
  straight away. Each run logs one `KevBoxHome` line per step reached.
- **AC-5** A run that still times out logs which window the service saw at its last poll: the
  package name, or `none`.
- **AC-6** With KevBox Home's accessibility service on, holding Volume up or Volume down inside any
  app keeps changing the volume until release; a single short press still changes it by one step.
- **AC-7** The repeat stops on the held key's key-up and when the service is interrupted or
  destroyed; pressing the other volume key while one is held switches the repeat to that key (as
  Android does with the service off); it never runs for any other key.
- **AC-8** Mapped keys (Netflix, Prime Video, YouTube, Home, Apps) behave as before; the existing
  `KeyFilterTest` passes unchanged.
- **AC-9** The clock and the weather sit top left, starting at the left column's x, at today's sizes
  and fonts, vertically centred with the top-right buttons. The top right keeps now playing, the
  Update chip and the buttons only.
- **AC-10** With all column rows fitting on screen, the block of app rows, divider and "More apps"
  is centred vertically on the 540dp canvas; with more rows than fit, the column scrolls as before.
- **AC-11** Focus routes are unchanged: Up from the first app reaches the first top-right button,
  Down from the buttons returns to the row it left, Down past the last app reaches "More apps".
- **AC-12** The Gate passes with zero lint errors and the release APK at most 1,048,576 bytes.
- **AC-13** `UPSTREAM-SYNC.md` has an up-to-date row for every upstream file this change touches.

## Non-goals
- A bigger clock or a date under it: Kevin chose today's sizes.
- Long-press or double-press mappings for other keys: unchanged non-goal from 0.1.
- Restart app on the TCL's Settings app: it is checked again by the next checklist run.
- Moving the top-right buttons: they stay where they are.

## Decisions
- Volume repeat is made by KevBox Home over making the service stop filtering volume keys, because
  an accessibility service that filters keys receives every key; there is no per-key opt-out.
- The repeat calls `AudioManager.adjustSuggestedStreamVolume(ADJUST_RAISE/LOWER,
  USE_DEFAULT_STREAM_TYPE, FLAG_SHOW_UI)` over re-injecting key events, because a service cannot
  inject keys, and this is the call Android's own volume-key handling ends in (it also reaches
  HDMI-CEC volume when the box forwards volume to the TV).
- Repeat timing comes from `ViewConfiguration.getKeyRepeatTimeout()` and `getKeyRepeatDelay()`, over
  fixed numbers, so it matches what the box does with the service off.
- Restart app reads the Settings window from `service.windows` (with
  `flagRetrieveInteractiveWindows`) over `rootInActiveWindow`, which returned nothing usable on the
  Xiaomi box (checklist run 2026-10-29: `step FIND timed out` with Force stop on screen).
- Settings pages start with `FLAG_ACTIVITY_NEW_TASK | FLAG_ACTIVITY_CLEAR_TASK` over plain
  `NEW_TASK`, so Android's Settings task never brings back a stale page.
- The column is centred on the whole canvas over centring between the clock and the bottom line,
  as Kevin asked ("middle of the left column").

### Task 1: Fresh Settings pages and a Restart app that works on Google TV
**Delivers:** AC-1, AC-2, AC-3, AC-4, AC-5, AC-13
**Files:** `app/src/main/java/cloud/samlo/couch/kevbox/buttons/ButtonRow.kt`,
`app/src/main/java/cloud/samlo/couch/kevbox/menu/AppMenu.kt`,
`app/src/main/java/cloud/samlo/couch/kevbox/restart/AppRestart.kt`,
`app/src/main/java/cloud/samlo/couch/kevbox/restart/ForceStop.kt`,
`app/src/main/res/xml/home_accessibility.xml`, `UPSTREAM-SYNC.md`,
`app/src/test/java/cloud/samlo/couch/kevbox/SettingsWindowTest.kt` (new)
**Interfaces:** `internal data class WindowRoot(val pkg: String?, val isApplication: Boolean, val root: Any)` and
`internal fun pickSettingsRoot(windows: List<WindowRoot>, active: WindowRoot?, settings: Set<String>): Any?`,
pure, so the window choice is JVM-testable. ForceStop builds the list from `service.windows`
(`isApplication` = `AccessibilityWindowInfo.TYPE_APPLICATION`) and `active` from `rootInActiveWindow`.
**Does:**
- Every Settings-page intent in these three files adds `FLAG_ACTIVITY_CLEAR_TASK` next to
  `FLAG_ACTIVITY_NEW_TASK` (ButtonRow.openSettings for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Settings; AppMenu's App
  info; AppRestart's details page).
- `home_accessibility.xml` adds `flagRetrieveInteractiveWindows` to `accessibilityFlags`.
- ForceStop reads its root through `pickSettingsRoot`: the first application window whose package is
  in `SETTINGS`, else the first window of any type whose package is in `SETTINGS`, else `active` when
  its package is in `SETTINGS` (this covers an empty window list too), else null. find(), confirm()
  and relaunch() all use it.
- Each step reached logs one line (`restart <pkg>: found Force stop`, `pressed OK`, `relaunched`,
  `already stopped, relaunched`); a timeout logs the package of the root it last saw, or `none`.
- `UPSTREAM-SYNC.md`: the `home_accessibility.xml` row mentions the new flag.
**Tests:** `SettingsWindowTest`: a settings application window is picked when it is not first and a
non-application settings window comes before it; `active` is used when the list is empty or has no
settings window; null when neither the list nor `active` belongs to a settings package.
**Verify:** `./gradlew --no-daemon :app:testDebugUnitTest --tests 'cloud.samlo.couch.kevbox.SettingsWindowTest' :app:compileDebugKotlin`

### Task 2: Held volume ramps with the service on
**Delivers:** AC-6, AC-7, AC-8, AC-13
**Files:** `app/src/main/java/cloud/samlo/couch/kevbox/keys/VolumeRepeat.kt` (new),
`app/src/main/java/cloud/samlo/couch/HomeAccessibilityService.kt`, `UPSTREAM-SYNC.md`,
`app/src/test/java/cloud/samlo/couch/kevbox/VolumeRepeatTest.kt` (new)
**Interfaces:** `class VolumeRepeat(schedule: (delayMs: Long, run: () -> Unit) -> Unit, cancel: () -> Unit, adjust: (raise: Boolean) -> Unit, timeoutMs: Long, delayMs: Long)` with
`fun onKey(action: Int, keyCode: Int, repeatCount: Int)` and `fun stop()`; plain values in, like KeyFilter.
**Does:**
- In `onKeyEvent`, before the KeyFilter, VOLUME_UP / VOLUME_DOWN events go to VolumeRepeat, which
  never consumes them (the system still handles the press itself, so one press = one step).
- On a volume key-down with repeatCount 0, VolumeRepeat schedules `adjust` after `timeoutMs`, then
  every `delayMs`, until that key's up; a down of the other volume key cancels the current repeat
  and starts one for the new key; an up of a key that is not the one repeating changes nothing.
- The service passes `ViewConfiguration.getKeyRepeatTimeout()`, `getKeyRepeatDelay()`, a main-thread
  Handler, and `AudioManager.adjustSuggestedStreamVolume(ADJUST_RAISE or ADJUST_LOWER,
  USE_DEFAULT_STREAM_TYPE, FLAG_SHOW_UI)`; it calls `stop()` in `onInterrupt` and `onDestroy`.
- `UPSTREAM-SYNC.md`: the `HomeAccessibilityService.kt` row mentions the volume repeat.
**Tests:** `VolumeRepeatTest`: no adjust before the timeout, then one per delay while held, and up stops
it; the other volume key's down switches the repeat to it; a non-volume key never schedules anything.
**Verify:** `./gradlew --no-daemon :app:testDebugUnitTest --tests 'cloud.samlo.couch.kevbox.VolumeRepeatTest' --tests 'cloud.samlo.couch.kevbox.KeyFilterTest' :app:compileDebugKotlin`
